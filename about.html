About

I’m Daniel Yoon, a Korean American husband, father, and pastor in Southern California. I’m still learning what each one means, and writing is where I work it out.

I’m a dad of two toddlers, and I’m turning forty next year, which I’m handling with all the calm you’d expect from a professional overthinker. And as I work out the issues in my heart, I write about the things people raised to keep quiet tend to carry alone: doubt, shame, and parenting burnout.

For about twelve years I’ve pastored and preached, mostly to young adults in the church. I earned my MDiv at Talbot School of Theology. I studied psychology in undergrad, and I still read psych research because I need it.
