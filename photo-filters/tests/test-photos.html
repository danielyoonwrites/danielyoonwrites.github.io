Photo Filters: a test photo

A made-up photo for checking Photo Filters on a computer. It's tall, and it's saved turned on its side with a note saying which way is up, the way a phone's camera saves a tall photo. It also carries a made-up location in the Pacific Ocean, for checking that exports leave the location out.
