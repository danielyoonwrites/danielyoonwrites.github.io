Read `PROGRESS.md` first (where it stands and the next step), then `DECISIONS.md` (choices and their reasons) and `SPEC.md`. Re-read the parts of `SPEC.md` a build step needs before starting it.

It lives in this folder and will be published at https://danielyoonwrites.github.io/family-activity-finder (not listed in search engines). ## Rules for working here

- Same rules as the website's `CLAUDE.md`: plain words for a beginner, one small change per pull request with a check the owner does, no build tools, phone first, screenshots before every pull request.
- Built to work for anyone, used just by the owner for now (his "A," November 5, 2026).
- Right before full: official calendars first, AI search second (his "A," November 5, 2026). Every item keeps its source link and when it was read. Check the year on every date, and convert every time to the area's own time. "Cost not stated" is never counted as free. When something can't be confirmed, the app says so.
- Posts by influencers count only when they're very current and match an official source (the owner's rule, November 5, 2026).
- Privacy: the repository and the website are public. Never put the owner's city, his kids' ages, names or schools, or a key in this folder, in a commit message, in a screenshot or in test data. The saved checks use made-up calendars and places. The test details are in his private Notion notes ("Family activity finder app (Nov 2026)," under Master Notes).
- Kids' ages, the budget and the day's plan stay on the phone (Claude's plan, in `SPEC.md`).
- Ask before anything that costs money (Cloudflare's paid plan, more AI use).
